Spain

CDTI opens €150 million INNTERCONECTA-STEP call on 17 November

Spain's regional R&D consortia call funds company-led projects in STEP technologies, with at least €10 million reserved for women's health.

Published 12 November 2026 · By Emilio Mulet · Source: Boletín Oficial del Estado (BOE) – CDTI call extract↗ (opens in a new tab)

Key facts

Programme
Consorcios Regionales INNTERCONECTA-STEP 2026 (CDTI, co-financed by ERDF 2021-2027)
Budget
€150 million maximum; at least €10 million reserved for women's health
Applications
17 November to 23 December 2026, 12:00 (peninsular time)
Project size
€1 million to €4 million eligible budget; at least €175,000 per company
Consortia
2 to 6 companies, including at least one SME
Duration
Start 1 January 2027; end 31 December 2028 or 30 June 2029

Spain's Official State Gazette (BOE) published on 12 November the extract of the 2026 INNTERCONECTA-STEP call, approved by the presidency of CDTI on 6 November. The call supports cooperative R&D projects led by companies in the strategic technology fields of the EU's STEP platform: digital technologies, clean and resource-efficient technologies, and biotechnologies. It has a maximum budget of €150 million and is co-financed by the European Regional Development Fund (ERDF) 2021-2027.

This edition sets aside at least €10 million for proposals addressing women's health, specifically conditions with a greater impact on women. If there are not enough fundable proposals in that strand, the unused amount can be allocated to other eligible projects according to their ranking.

Applicants must form groups of two to six companies, at least two of them independent from each other and at least one an SME. No company, or set of linked companies, may hold more than 70% of the eligible budget, and each entity may appear in no more than two applications. The group has no legal personality, so one member acts as coordinator, and the partners must sign an internal agreement before applying. At least 10% of the eligible budget must be subcontracted to research organisations.

The work must take place in at least two of these regions: Andalucía, Illes Balears, Canarias, Castilla-La Mancha, Castilla y León, Comunidad Valenciana, Extremadura, Galicia and Región de Murcia. Projects are multi-year, with an eligible budget between €1 million and €4 million, a minimum of €175,000 per company, and a start date of 1 January 2027. Applications open on 17 November and close on 23 December 2026 at noon, peninsular time.

Why it matters

With a short window between opening and closing, and a consortium agreement required before submission, the real constraint is partner building, not writing. Consortia need companies active in at least two of the eligible regions plus a research organisation ready to take a meaningful subcontract, so groups that already work together have a clear head start. The women's health reserve is worth a close look for medtech, biotech and digital health firms: a ring-fenced amount can mean less competition than in the general pool. Companies should also watch the limit of two applications per entity and the 70% ceiling per partner when deciding which consortia to join and how to split the budget.
