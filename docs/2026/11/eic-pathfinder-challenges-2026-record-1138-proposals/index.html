EIC Pathfinder Challenges 2026 draws record 1,138 proposals

Submissions almost doubled on 2025 for an indicative €96 million budget across three challenges, with results expected in spring 2027.

Published 10 November 2026 · By Emilio Mulet · Source: European Innovation Council↗ (opens in a new tab)

Key facts

Proposals
1,138 (almost double 2025)
Participants
5,107 from 58 countries
Indicative budget
€96 million
Grant size
Up to €4 million (TRL 3–4)
Deadline
28 October 2026 (closed)
Results
Expected in spring 2027

The European Innovation Council's 2026 Pathfinder Challenges call, which closed on 28 October 2026, received 1,138 project proposals, almost twice as many as in 2025. The applications involve 5,107 participants from 58 countries.

Demand was spread unevenly across the three challenges. The challenge on deep reasoning, abstraction and planning for trustworthy cognitive AI systems (DeepRAP) attracted 450 proposals, biotechnology for healthy ageing received 427, and advanced materials for miniaturised energy harvesting systems received 261.

The call has an indicative budget of €96 million, expected to be shared roughly equally between the three challenges. Pathfinder grants of up to €4 million fund early-stage research up to proof of concept or laboratory validation (TRL 3–4). Selected projects can also work with EIC Programme Managers and may receive additional funding to explore innovation potential or join portfolio activities.

Evaluation has started and the EIC expects to announce results in spring 2027.

Why it matters

A near doubling of applications for a fixed budget means much tougher competition, so a strong concept is no longer enough on its own: proposals need a clear fit with the specific challenge and a credible path from research to proof of concept. The AI and healthy-ageing challenges were the most crowded. Teams planning a Pathfinder bid in the next cycle should check the EIC 2027 work programme early and consider whether an open Pathfinder or a less contested challenge better matches their project.
