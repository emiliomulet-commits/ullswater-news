AI Office seeks up to 100 European AI providers for SMEs via EDIHs

European AI providers with market-ready solutions can apply until 17 December 2026 to join an 'EDIH Ready' catalogue that Digital Innovation Hubs will offer to SMEs.

Published 17 November 2026 · By Emilio Mulet · Source: European Commission – Shaping Europe's digital future↗ (opens in a new tab)

Key facts

Initiative
Call for expression of interest under the Apply AI Strategy
Places
Up to 100 AI providers in the first edition
Deadline
17 December 2026, 17:00 CET (may close earlier)
Eligibility
Mature AI solutions; providers headquartered in the EU or Digital Europe associated countries

Submit an expression of interest ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The European Commission's AI Office opened on 17 November 2026 a call for expression of interest for European AI providers that want to present mature AI solutions to the network of European Digital Innovation Hubs (EDIHs). The goal, set out in the Apply AI Strategy, is to bridge supply and demand by helping SMEs across Europe adopt European AI, with EDIHs acting as trusted intermediaries. The hubs have already delivered services to more than 50,000 organisations.

The first edition is open to up to 100 providers. Solutions must be ready for deployment in SMEs: proofs of concept, research projects, minimum viable products and solutions not yet on the market are not suitable. Eligibility depends on where the provider is headquartered, which must be an EU Member State or a country associated to the Digital Europe Programme. Submissions are made in English through an EU Survey form by 17 December 2026 at 17:00 CET, and the call may close earlier if 100 suitable solutions arrive first.

Selected solutions will form a catalogue on the AI-on-Demand Platform, listed in the Business Navigator with an 'EDIH Ready' label. Details of suitable candidates will be shared with DeployAI, the EU-funded consortium behind the platform, which will handle onboarding, and information sessions with EDIHs are planned for the first quarter of 2027.

Why it matters

Ullswater Investments analysis: this is not a grant, but it gives European AI companies a direct channel to SME customers through a network the Commission funds, which can shorten sales cycles and add credibility. Because places are limited and the call can close early, providers with a product already deployed at customers should apply soon and present clear evidence of market readiness. Visibility in the catalogue may also help when preparing proposals for future Digital Europe or Horizon Europe uptake actions that involve EDIHs.
