Spain's State Research Agency opens €10 million call for R&D technical and management staff

Research bodies, universities, health research institutes and technology centres can apply until 17 December for three-year contracts.

Published 25 November 2026 · By Emilio Mulet · Source: Agencia Estatal de Investigación (AEI)↗ (opens in a new tab)

Key facts

Call
2026 grants for R&D&I technical and management staff (AEI)
Budget
Up to €10 million (€235,200 reserved for people with a disability of 33% or more)
Deadlines
Candidates: 10 December; institutions: 17 December 2026, 14:00
Eligible
Public research bodies, universities, National Health System bodies, accredited health research institutes, State technology centres, other non-profit R&D centres
Aid
€19,600/year (graduates) or €16,300/year (higher technicians), for 3 years

Spain's State Research Agency has opened its 2026 call to fund contracts for technical and R&D&I management staff, with a budget of up to €10 million. Research bodies, universities, health research institutes, technology centres and other non-profit R&D centres can apply.

Candidates must register by 10 December and institutions must submit applications by 17 December 2026 at 14:00. The grant covers three-year contracts at €19,600 per year for graduates or €16,300 per year for higher technicians.

Why it matters

Ullswater Investments analysis: project management and technical support staff are a frequent bottleneck when research centres prepare and run Horizon Europe projects. This call helps them strengthen those teams.

Official source

Agencia Estatal de Investigación (AEI)↗ (opens in a new tab) — always check eligibility, budgets and deadlines on the official page before applying.
