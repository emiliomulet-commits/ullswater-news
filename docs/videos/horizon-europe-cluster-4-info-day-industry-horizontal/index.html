Horizon Europe Cluster 4 Info Day: Industry & Horizontal Topics

HaDEA's info day on the industry topics of Cluster 4 (Digital, Industry and Space) in the 2026-2027 work programme, plus the application process.

Published 19 October 2026 · Video by HaDEA (European Health and Digital Executive Agency)↗ (opens in a new tab)

Embedded from YouTube. Watch on YouTube ↗ (opens in a new tab)

This recording is the second day of the autumn Horizon Europe Cluster 4 Info Days, held on 15 October 2026 and published by the European Health and Digital Executive Agency (HaDEA). It presents the industry and horizontal topics of the 2026-2027 work programme.

The agenda covers materials and production (advanced manufacturing, circular advanced materials, textile circularity and the decarbonisation of energy-intensive industries), critical raw materials, and innovation and valorisation topics such as academic intellectual assets, pilot access to technology infrastructures, proof-of-market schemes and venture builder networks.

Horizontal sessions explain the application process, partial randomisation, central validation by REA, legal and financial aspects, lump sums, the integration of social sciences and humanities, and the services of National Contact Points.

Why watch it

Ullswater Investments note: info days are the most direct way to hear how the Commission reads its own topics. For manufacturers, materials companies and technology centres preparing a Cluster 4 proposal, the topic presentations and the sessions on lump sums and evaluation are worth watching before drafting.
